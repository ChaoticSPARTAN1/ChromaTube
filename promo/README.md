# Promo

A short promo for ChromaTube: your own video channels on the Chromatic, from your own Jellyfin server
over Wi-Fi.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `chromatube-1080x1080.mp4` | Promo, square (14 s) |
| `chromatube-1920x1080.mp4` | Promo, wide (14 s) |
| `chromatube-still-1600x900.png` | Still picture |

All videos are MP4 (H.264, 30 fps, AAC sound). Stream quality on the console may vary.
